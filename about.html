Who We Are

A Place Born From
the River's Edge

Kampot Riverside Resort began as a dream shared by a small family who grew up along the banks of the Praek Tuek Chhu river. We wanted to create a place where guests could slow down, breathe in the salt-tinged air, and truly feel the soul of Kampot.

Opened in 2012, the resort has grown from four bungalows into a full boutique property — yet it has never lost the intimacy and warmth that defined it from the very first day.

Our Philosophy

Slow Travel,
Real Connection

We believe the best travel memories aren't made by rushing through itineraries — they're found in a quiet morning coffee while watching river mist clear, or in a long conversation with a local fisherman about the tides.

Every detail at Kampot Riverside is designed to invite you to pause: handcrafted furniture from local artisans, an open-air dining terrace that follows the river breeze, and a team that genuinely wants to share the best of our home province with you.

"We don't just offer a room — we offer a reason to stay a little longer."
— Sok Dara, Founder, Kampot Riverside Resort

What We Stand For

Our Values

Local at Heart

We source ingredients from Kampot's farms, employ local staff, and partner with community artisans. The resort is an extension of the town we love.

Sustainable Stays

Solar panels power our common areas, rainwater is harvested for the garden, and single-use plastics have been eliminated across the entire property.

Genuine Warmth

Hospitality here is never scripted. Our team greets every guest as a friend visiting our home — because to us, that is exactly what you are.
