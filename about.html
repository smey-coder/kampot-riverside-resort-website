Welcome to Your Perfect Getaway

Welcome to Kampot Riverside Resort, a peaceful riverside sanctuary located in the heart of beautiful Kampot Province, Cambodia. Surrounded by lush tropical gardens and overlooking the calm Preaek Tuek Chhu River, our resort offers guests a perfect escape into nature, comfort, and authentic Khmer hospitality.


Kampot is one of Cambodia’s most attractive tourist destinations, famous for its peaceful atmosphere, colonial architecture, stunning mountain scenery, and world-famous Kampot pepper. Visitors can enjoy relaxing river cruises, explore Bokor Mountain, visit local pepper farms, discover salt fields, and experience traditional Cambodian culture.
